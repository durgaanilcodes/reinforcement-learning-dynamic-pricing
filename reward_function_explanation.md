# Reward Function Explanation

## What is a Reward Function?

A reward function measures how good an action is after it is performed by the reinforcement learning agent.

The objective of the agent is to maximize the total reward.

---

## Dynamic Pricing Example

State:

- High demand
- Weekend
- High occupancy

Action:

Increase room price.

Reward:

Higher revenue.

---

Another Example

State:

Low demand

Action:

Increase price significantly.

Reward:

Negative reward because bookings decrease.

---

## Reward Function Goals

- Maximize revenue.
- Maintain occupancy.
- Balance customer satisfaction.
- Learn the best pricing strategy.

---

## Importance

The reward function guides the learning process and helps the agent determine which pricing decisions are beneficial.
