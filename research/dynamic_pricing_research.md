# Dynamic Pricing Research

## What is Dynamic Pricing?

Dynamic pricing is a pricing strategy in which prices are adjusted automatically based on factors such as demand, inventory, time, competition, and seasonality.

## Travel Industry

Airlines use dynamic pricing to maximize ticket revenue by considering:

- Remaining seats
- Days before departure
- Customer demand
- Holidays
- Competitor pricing

## Hospitality Industry

Hotels adjust room prices depending on:

- Occupancy rate
- Weekend demand
- Festival seasons
- Local events
- Competitor prices

## Benefits

- Maximizes revenue
- Improves occupancy
- Reduces unsold inventory
- Enhances business profitability
