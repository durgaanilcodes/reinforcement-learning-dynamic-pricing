# Week 3 Technical Documentation

## Project
Reinforcement Learning Dynamic Pricing

---

## Week 3 Objectives

- Understand the Reinforcement Learning training process.
- Compare pricing before and after RL optimization.
- Study and compare RL algorithms.
- Analyze evaluation metrics used to assess model performance.
- Document the technical progress of Week 3.

---

## Tasks Completed

### 1. Reinforcement Learning Training Workflow
Documented the complete RL training process, including environment initialization, agent interaction, action selection, reward calculation, and policy learning.

### 2. Pricing Before vs After RL Optimization
Prepared a comparison explaining how Reinforcement Learning improves pricing decisions by maximizing long-term revenue while maintaining customer demand.

### 3. Algorithm Comparison
Compared Q-Learning, SARSA, and Deep Q-Network (DQN) based on their working principles, advantages, limitations, and suitability for dynamic pricing.

### 4. Evaluation Metrics
Documented the evaluation metrics used to assess reinforcement learning performance, including cumulative reward, average reward, convergence, revenue improvement, and booking rate.
