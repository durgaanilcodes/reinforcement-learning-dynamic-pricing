Week 2 Visualization Documentation



\## Objective



The objective of Week 2 was to prepare graphical representations that explain the relationship between pricing, demand, revenue, and booking probability for the Travel and Hospitality Dynamic Pricing project.



\## Visualizations



\### Demand vs Price



Illustrates that customer demand decreases as ticket prices increase.



\### Revenue vs Price



Shows that revenue is maximized at an optimal ticket price.



\### Booking Probability



Demonstrates that booking probability decreases with higher prices.



\## Workflow Diagrams



Two workflow diagrams were created:



\- Reinforcement Learning Workflow

\- Q-Learning Workflow



These diagrams explain how an RL agent interacts with the environment to learn optimal pricing decisions.
