\# Week 3 Visualization Report



\## Project



Travel \& Hospitality – Reinforcement Learning for Dynamic Pricing



\---



\## Objective



This report documents the visualizations created during Week 3 to analyse the training performance of the Reinforcement Learning pricing agent.



The visualizations include:



\- Reward vs Episode

\- Price vs Demand

\- Revenue Comparison

\- Training Progress

\- Moving Average Reward

\- Policy Improvement



The graphs are prepared using simulated training data and will be updated with actual RL training outputs when available.
