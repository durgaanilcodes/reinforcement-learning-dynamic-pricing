# Reinforcement Learning: State, Action and Reward

## State

A state represents the current situation of the environment.

Example in Dynamic Pricing

- Current hotel occupancy
- Season
- Customer demand
- Competitor prices

The agent observes the state before making a decision.

---

## Action

An action is the decision taken by the agent.

Examples

- Increase price
- Decrease price
- Maintain current price

---

## Reward

Reward is the feedback received after taking an action.

Examples

Positive Reward

- Higher revenue
- More bookings
- Better occupancy

Negative Reward

- Low occupancy
- Customer loss
- Reduced profit

---

## Example

State:
Weekend + High Demand + 90% Occupancy

↓

Action:
Increase room price by 10%

↓

Reward:
Higher revenue

The agent learns that increasing prices during high demand gives better rewards.

---

## Importance

State helps understand the current situation.

Action determines the pricing decision.

Reward helps the agent learn the best pricing strategy.
