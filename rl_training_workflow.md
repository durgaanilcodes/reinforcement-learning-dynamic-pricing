# Reinforcement Learning Training Workflow

## Step 1

Create the RL Environment using Gymnasium.

---

## Step 2

Initialize the agent.

---

## Step 3

Observe the current state.

Example:

- Hotel occupancy
- Season
- Customer demand

---

## Step 4

Choose an action.

Possible actions:

- Increase price
- Decrease price
- Maintain price

---

## Step 5

Execute the action.

---

## Step 6

Receive reward.

Examples:

Positive reward:
- Higher revenue
- Better occupancy

Negative reward:
- Low bookings
- Revenue loss

---

## Step 7

Update the learning algorithm.

---

## Step 8

Repeat the process for multiple episodes until the agent learns the optimal pricing strategy.

---

## Outcome

The trained agent learns to maximize long-term revenue while maintaining customer demand.
